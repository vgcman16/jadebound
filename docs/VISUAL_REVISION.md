# Visual revision 1 — historical checkpoint

This records the first environment/rigid-part art revision before the modular skinned hero was added. It is retained as historical progress, not a description of the latest character pipeline. See [current visible equipment](EQUIPMENT.md) and [modular hero source](../art/MODULAR_HERO_GUIDE.md).

Changed in-engine:
- Layered slate roofs with overlapping tile courses, upturned eaves, timber brackets, deep lattice windows and stone footings
- Adult-proportioned topknot wanderer, layered coat/armor, scabbard and steel saber with restrained jade inset
- Flattened irregular cherry-blossom branch pads with angular petal breakup and gaps; muted rose palette
- Continuous natural ground shading, asymmetrical fitted courtyard stones and a partially buried irregular flagstone lane
- Softer/lighter shadow footprint and subtle character contact shadows
- Closer isometric framing, less intrusive HUD and short tapered skill trails

At this historical checkpoint, remaining art work included: credible articulated anatomy/limbs, smoother head/hand/boot shapes, authored anticipation/impact/recovery poses, more varied world materials and higher-quality character silhouettes. Its rigid-piece character animation and angular figure construction were prototype limitations. The subsequent modular checkpoint replaces the playable hero with a native skinned rig; further material and attack-pose quality work remains in progress.

Preview provenance: stills are saved directly by the running Godot viewport. The short movie is recorded by Godot Movie Maker with automated player input that goes through the normal authoritative world simulation. It is labelled as an automated in-engine demo. It is not concept art or a prerendered promise of future graphics.
