# Original courtyard texture provenance

The stone, wood, plaster and earth512px albedo/normal/roughness maps are generated by `tools/generate_courtyard_detail.py` from original non-periodic multiscale fields and material-specific grain/cavity treatment. The Blender source embeds the maps; the editable PNG assets also ship under `game/assets/textures/courtyard/`.

`jb_meadow_albedo.png` was generated specifically for this original game with the built-in image generation tool on2026-10-02. It is a base-color terrain asset, not a screenshot or a representation of completed gameplay. No source-game pixels or user reference photos were supplied to the generator. It is sampled on the actual engine terrain, and final acceptance requires an in-engine capture.

Prompt: Original seamless square top-down albedo for a3D martial-fantasy game's grassy terrain. Dense fine olive meadow grass with short irregular slender blades and overlapping tufts, scattered dry tan blades, small mossy gaps and subdued bare-soil flecks, a few tiny pebbles. Approximately2×2m physical coverage. Muted olive, moss, dry ochre and grey-brown soil. Straight overhead, even diffuse illumination, no directional cast shadows/vignette/specular/horizon/perspective/large objects/flowers/characters/labels/UI/frame/grid. Seamless edges. Output only the base-color tile. Original artwork; do not copy any specific game's texture.

The limestone source image and its full generation prompt are recorded in `texture_sources.json`; its edited face atlas, normal/roughness masks and side-face UV mapping are authored in the courtyard generator.

The live prototype/courtyard geometry remains original Blender-authored source. The separate human-base/concept-warden authoring studies derive their anatomy and rig from verified MakeHuman core CC0 assets, documented in their own guides; they are not yet the live gameplay hero. Copyrighted source-game reference imagery is excluded from game assets and the public repository. This note records provenance and does not establish an open-source license for the project.
